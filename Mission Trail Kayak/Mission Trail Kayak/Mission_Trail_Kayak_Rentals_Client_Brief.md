# Mission Trail Kayak Rentals — Mini Client Brief
## Instructor Demonstration Client

### Organization
Mission Trail Kayak Rentals is a small San Antonio-area outdoor recreation business offering guided and self-guided kayak rentals near the Mission Reach of the San Antonio River.

### Business situation
The current online presence is mostly social media and a simple map listing. Customers repeatedly message or call with the same questions before visiting.

### Client goals
The client wants a small website that helps visitors:
- understand rental options;
- know where to meet;
- know what to bring;
- understand basic safety expectations;
- contact the business with questions.

### Audience notes
- Many visitors are tourists or first-time paddlers.
- The client reports that most customers check information on a phone.
- Beginners often ask whether prior kayaking experience is required.
- Some visitors arrive unsure about the meeting location or what equipment is supplied.

### Content the client can provide
- rental option descriptions;
- basic safety rules;
- meeting-location instructions;
- seasonal hours;
- short guide biographies;
- “what to bring” information;
- beginner FAQ answers;
- contact information.

### Content-management note
Seasonal hours change several times per year. Staff would prefer to update those hours without asking a developer.

### Interaction idea
The FAQ may benefit from a simple reveal/accordion interaction so phone users can scan questions before opening answers.

### Client comment that requires investigation
> “Maybe customer accounts would be cool someday so people could save their information.”

The client has **not** described:
- account-specific user tasks;
- saved reservations;
- payment processing;
- membership benefits;
- authentication requirements;
- a launch requirement for customer accounts.

### Constraints
- Keep the first release realistic for a small local business.
- Mobile usability is important.
- Accessibility is required.
- The site should remain maintainable after launch.
- No production build is part of this Week 13 demonstration.

### Open questions
The brief does not establish:
- whether online booking will be part of the course project;
- whether the business already has a CMS/hosting platform;
- final photography availability;
- exact seasonal-hour update workflow.

Treat these as questions/risks unless additional evidence is supplied.
