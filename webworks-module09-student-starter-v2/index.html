Plan Your Visit

Know before you go.

The market is designed for a relaxed Saturday visit. Most guests spend between 60 and 90 minutes browsing.

Parking
Free parking is available in the east lot. Accessible spaces are closest to the main entrance.
Food
Two local food vendors and a coffee cart will be open during market hours.
Payment
Most vendors accept cards and digital payments. Some also accept cash.
Accessibility
The venue has a step-free entrance, accessible restrooms, and seating throughout the hall.
